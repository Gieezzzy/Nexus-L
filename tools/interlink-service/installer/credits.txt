Nexus-L InterLink Sender
Credited by Systems & Process Audit

This installs the Nexus-L InterLink Service: a small tray program that receives
approved reports from Nexus-L and uploads them to InterLink in the background,
one after the other.

After the installation:
  1. Open the tray icon > Settings and check the Nexus-L address and the service key.
  2. Sign in to InterLink once (the "Sign in" option).
  3. Leave the program running - it starts with Windows if you ticked that option.

Updating over an existing installation keeps your settings and your InterLink sign-in.
